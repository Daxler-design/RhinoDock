## MonoCode

Source: https://github.com/hardbeat920/monocode

rhDock adapts MonoCode's harness contract, registry, protocol mapping, tool naming, and paced text reveal (`frontend/src/usePacedText.ts`). The C# adapters are ports for rhDock's host; OpenCode targets its 2.x API.

## RhinoAI

Source: https://github.com/mcneel/rhinoai

The agent process launcher retains patterns from RhinoAI’s `CliProcess`. The integrated Rhino 8 external tools, resources, parameter binding, and dispatch code are adapted from commit `46756c0f09c135e86e0748c8ae21929252402039`. Sources, license, hashes, and local adaptations are recorded in `src/rhDock.Rhino/RhinoAI/UPSTREAM.md`. The previous vendored ACP schema and transport are removed.
